**Thread title suggestion:** [Resource] MyPokePanion — free dex, maps, Nuzlocke tracker & battle tools (EN/DE)

**Body:**

Sharing a fan project I've been building for my own playthroughs — might be useful for others too.

**MyPokePanion** (https://mypokepanion.com/en) tries to put the stuff I always had in separate tabs into one place:

- National dex (Gen 1–9)
- Region maps with wild encounter tables — handy for ROM hacks when you want reference data
- Nuzlocke tracker (offline solo + optional multiplayer)
- Team builder with legality checks and Showdown format
- Versus lab — 1v1 damage calc *and* a turn-by-turn fight simulator from the same matchup

English and German. Free, no ads, fan project (not affiliated with Nintendo/The Pokémon Company).

Still in active development. If something's missing for your runs, there's a feedback page on the site.

Happy to answer questions.
