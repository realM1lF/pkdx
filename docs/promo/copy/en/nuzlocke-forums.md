**Thread title suggestion:** MyPokePanion — free Nuzlocke tracker with maps, multiplayer, calc & 1v1 battle sim (EN/DE)

**Body:**

Hey everyone,

I've been running Nuzlockes for a while. At some point I got tired of six browser tabs for every decision — wiki for encounters, type chart, damage calc, route list, team sheet. You know the drill.

So I built **MyPokePanion**: one site for what I actually need at the desk while playing.

What's in there:

- **Nuzlocke tracker** — solo (offline, localStorage) or multiplayer with invite codes and live sync
- **Region maps** with encounter data (Kanto through Unova, schematic + original overworld view)
- **Full dex** (Gen 1–9) with sprites, moves, evolutions
- **Team builder** with legality checks and Showdown import/export
- **Versus lab** — gen-accurate damage calc *plus* a **1v1 fight simulator** you can run from the same matchup (turn-by-turn, not just numbers on a spreadsheet)
- **German and English** — whole UI, not just dex names

Fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo. Free, no ads. I keep adding stuff in spare time.

If you try it and something feels off — missing feature, bug, weird UX — I'd like to hear it. Feedback page on the site goes straight to GitHub issues.

**https://mypokepanion.com/en**

Happy to answer questions here.
